## DELIVERABLE 5 — NEWSLETTER ATTRIBUTION LINE
*Appears at the TOP of each mirrored newsletter page.*

> This piece first appeared in Fab Dolan's newsletter on Substack. [Subscribe for new issues →](https://fabdolan.substack.com)

**Placement: TOP.** A reader arriving via search has no context for what they're reading or who wrote it. Attribution at the top establishes authorship and origin before they invest time in the piece. Bottom placement reads like a legal disclaimer. Top placement reads like an editorial note.

## DELIVERABLE 7 — SHARE SECTION LABEL
*Appears before share buttons on article, case study, and newsletter pages.*

**Keep as:** `Share`

The colon-prefix system is for category labels within the site's navigation logic. Applied to a share action it would read as odd. "Share" as plain text is correct.

---

## DELIVERABLE 8 — RELATED READING SECTION LABEL
*Appears above 2–3 related articles at the bottom of each article page.*

**Keep as:** `:RELATED READING`

Accurate, uses the brand mark correctly, doesn't need to be clever. Confirm it renders in uppercase Space Mono — if currently sentence case, it should be all-caps to match label treatment used elsewhere on the site.
